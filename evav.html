WHAT IS EV AND AV?

Two technologies, one mobility shift

Electric vehicles change what powers our roads. Automated vehicles change how we use them.

The Basics

Automated Vehicles (AVs)

AI-driven: Uses sensors, cameras, and software to perform driving tasks (UCF Online, 2025).
Self-operating: Senses its surroundings and adjusts speed or direction with limited or no human intervention (UCF Online, 2025).
Core goal: Improve road safety, traffic flow, and transportation efficiency (UCF Online, 2025; Earth.Org, 2022).

Electric Vehicles (EVs)

Electric drivetrain: Uses electric motors powered by rechargeable batteries instead of gasoline or diesel (Babylon2k, 2025).
Energy storage: Charges from the electrical grid and stores energy onboard (Babylon2k, 2025).
Core goal: Cut oil dependence and eliminate tailpipe emissions (Recharged, n.d.).

Levels of Automation

The Society of Automotive Engineers defines six levels (UCF Online, 2025).

0No automation
The driver does everything.
1Driver assistance
Lane-keeping or cruise control.
2Partial automation
Under driver supervision.
3Conditional
May need human assistance.
4High automation
In specific areas, like city zones.
5Full automation
Drives itself in all conditions.

Most cars on the road today are Level 2, and Level 5 remains a long-term goal (UCF Online, 2025).

How an AV Works

1

Sensors

Cameras, radar, and LIDAR detect lanes, obstacles, and other vehicles (UCF Online, 2025).

2

Processing

AI software analyzes the data and predicts how nearby objects will move (UCF Online, 2025).

3

Actuators

The car automatically brakes, steers, and accelerates as needed (UCF Online, 2025).

Why EVs Matter

Lower emissionsLower total greenhouse gas emissions than combustion vehicles, even counting electricity generation (Recharged, n.d.).
Cleaner airNo tailpipe nitrogen oxides or particulate matter in cities (Recharged, n.d.).
Energy efficiencyElectric motors turn more energy into motion than combustion engines (Recharged, n.d.).
Less oilLower oil demand reduces dependence on oil-producing regions (Recharged, n.d.).
Quieter streetsReduced noise pollution in urban areas (Recharged, n.d.).
Second lifeRetired batteries can be reused in stationary energy storage (Recharged, n.d.).

EV Challenges in the Philippines

High upfront costEVs still cost more than traditional vehicles, though prices are coming down (Babylon2k, 2025).
Limited chargingOutside major cities, charging stations are still scarce (Babylon2k, 2025).
Range anxietyMany drivers worry about running out of battery mid-journey (Babylon2k, 2025).
Longer refuelingCharging takes longer than filling a gas tank (Babylon2k, 2025).
Battery replacementReplacement can be expensive, though most batteries have 8–10 year warranties (Babylon2k, 2025).
Public awarenessUnderstanding of EV ownership and maintenance is still limited (Babylon2k, 2025).
