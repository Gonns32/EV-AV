Our Advocacy

1

Awareness

Informing communities about the transition to clean transit, from global EV trends to local public transport modernization, including EV buses, jeepneys, and delivery fleets (Babylon2k, 2025).

2

Critical Evaluation

Weighing zero tailpipe emissions (Recharged, n.d.) against trade-offs such as battery mineral mining (Yucel, 2025) and charging from fossil-fuel-heavy grids (Earth.Org, 2022).

3

Ethical Dialogue

Exploring the safety, ethical, and regulatory challenges of driverless technology (University of Central Florida [UCF] Online, 2025).

EVs change what powers our roads, while AVs change how we use our roads. Combining both into Shared Autonomous Electric Vehicles (SAEVs) can cut per-mile greenhouse gas emissions by 87–94% (Greenblatt & Saxena, 2015, as cited in Yucel, 2025).

Did You Know?
